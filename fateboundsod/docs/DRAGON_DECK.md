# Dragon Convergence — NEW!

Select **Dragon** under Choose Your Element, or in the multiplayer deck selector. Available as an AI opponent too. Dragon is a deck selection, not a new gameplay element: all cards keep their original elements and effects.

Commander: **Golden Dragon King** (`CTRL_DRG_0001`), Light, 5 shards, 1 AP / 12 CH. This is the sole starting Controller; no reserve Controllers are in the deck. Royal Command costs 2 shards and gives one allied Dragon or Wyrm creature +2 AP until End Phase, once per owner turn. Existing attack protection and turn restrictions still apply.

30 cards, plus the commander:

- 14 creatures: two each of Frost Wyrm, Moss Dragon, Emperor’s Fire Dragon, Flame Dragon, Light Dragon, Supreme Water Dragon, and Wind Dragon.
- 12 spells: Ice Breath, White Out, Dragon’s Tsunami, Whirlpool, Burning Blast, Light Bind, Nature’s Blade, Soul Siphon, Dark Dimension, Wolf Lightning Strike, Spell Release, Creature Recall.
- 4 artifacts: Earth Staff, Blood Pendant, Spark Knight Blade, Wind Sword.

All nine existing elements are represented. Existing named-card requirements remain: for example, Emperor’s Fire Dragon does not gain Flame Emperor’s extra attack from Golden Dragon King. No free casting or universal element bonuses are granted. This is an initial playtest deck, not a statistically proven balance adjustment.

Artwork: built-in image generation, `public/cards/golden-dragon-king.png`. Prompt: premium dark-fantasy portrait of a crowned golden dragon on an obsidian throne above clouds, surrounded by nine subtle elemental motes, painterly cinematic gold lighting, no text or frame.
